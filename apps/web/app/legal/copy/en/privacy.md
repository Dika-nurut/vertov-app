# Privacy Policy and Personal Data Processing

_Revision of 20 June 2026_

> This English version is provided for convenience. The legally binding text is the Russian-language [Privacy Policy](https://vertov.space/legal/privacy?lang=ru).

## Preamble

This Privacy Policy governs how personal data is collected, stored, used, transferred and protected when using the AI image, video and audio generation service **Vertov** ([vertov.space](https://vertov.space)). It is based on Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data" ("152-FZ") and other applicable Russian regulations. By using the Service, you confirm you have read this Policy and consent to the processing of your personal data on its terms.

## 1. General

1.1. The data operator is the individual entrepreneur **Diyor Rovshanovich Nurutdinov** (OGRNIP 326774600070058) (the "Operator"). Full details are in Section 14 and on the [Requisites](https://vertov.space/legal/requisites) page.

1.2. The Operator independently determines the purposes and scope of processing and is responsible for compliance with 152-FZ.

1.3. The Policy applies to all users; processing is performed both with and without automation.

## 2. Personal Data Processed

2.1. The Operator processes only data necessary to run the Service and fulfil obligations to the user:

- **Identification & contact:** email; phone (for phone sign-in); display name; external account ID for Yandex ID / VK ID sign-in.
- **Account data:** interface language, settings, plan, Token balance.
- **Usage data:** prompt text, user-uploaded images and materials, generation history and results, timestamps.
- **Technical data:** session IP, browser/device (User-Agent) — collected automatically only for security, abuse prevention and diagnostics.
- **Payment data:** payment-method type and last digits, order amount and status. Full card details are **neither collected nor stored** by the Operator — they are handled by the payment service (Section 7).

  2.2. The Operator does **not** process special categories of personal data and does not collect them deliberately. Users must not enter such data into prompts.

  2.3. Onboarding answers are processed in anonymised form and are not used to identify a person.

## 3. Purposes of Processing

- **Operating the Service:** registration and authentication; processing requests and producing generations; Token accounting; personalisation.
- **Support and improvement:** stability monitoring; handling support requests; aggregated, anonymised usage analysis.
- **Legal compliance:** accounting and tax records for paid services; responding to lawful requests from authorities.
- **Communication:** service notices; marketing messages only with the user's consent and with an opt-out at any time.

## 4. Legal Bases (Art. 6(1) 152-FZ)

- **Performance of the contract** to which the user is a party (cl. 5) — to deliver the services under the [Public Offer](https://vertov.space/legal/offer).
- **The user's consent** (cl. 1) — for marketing and other consent-based purposes; revocable at any time (Section 9).
- **The Operator's legitimate interests** (cl. 7) — security and abuse prevention, respecting the user's rights.
- **Legal obligations** (cl. 2) — accounting and tax records.

## 5. Retention

- **Account and usage data** — for the life of the account; deleted within **30 days** of account deletion (except data the law requires retaining).
- **Support requests** — up to **6 months** after closure.
- **Payment/accounting data** — at least **5 years** per Russian law.
- **Marketing contacts** — until consent is withdrawn; thereafter kept on a suppression list to prevent re-sending.

On expiry, data is deleted or anonymised.

## 6. Cross-Border Transfer

6.1. To generate content, prompt text and user-uploaded materials are sent to AI model providers (including BytePlus, AtlasCloud) whose servers may be outside Russia. Error data (without prompt content) may be sent to a monitoring service.

6.2. Cross-border transfer is performed per Art. 12 of 152-FZ. The Operator minimises transferred data and does not send direct identifiers (email, phone) within generation requests.

6.3. Initial collection and storage of Russian citizens' personal data uses databases located **in the Russian Federation** (Art. 18(5) 152-FZ).

## 7. Disclosure to Third Parties

The Operator does not disclose personal data except to: the **Tochka Bank acquiring service** for settlements and fiscal receipts; **infrastructure and AI-model providers** as needed to deliver the service (Section 6); and **authorities** on lawful request. Processors are bound by confidentiality and data-protection law.

## 8. Localisation and Security

The Operator applies legal, organisational and technical safeguards: TLS in transit; access control and logging; storing identifying data separately from application data. Databases with Russian citizens' personal data are hosted on servers in the Russian Federation.

## 9. Your Rights (Art. 14–21 152-FZ)

You may: obtain information about and access to your data; require correction, blocking or deletion of incomplete, outdated, inaccurate or unlawfully processed data; **withdraw consent**; require processing to stop and data to be deleted; and complain to Roskomnadzor or a court. You can also **export your data** in machine-readable form and delete your account in settings. Requests go to **[privacy@vertov.space](mailto:privacy@vertov.space)** and are handled within **30 days**. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing or processing on other legal bases (e.g. tax records).

## 10. Automated Processing and AI

The Service uses automated systems, including AI models, to produce generations at the user's request. The Operator does **not** make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects on the user solely by automated processing of personal data.

## 11. Cookies

Only strictly necessary authentication cookies (a session token) are used. Web analytics is anonymised, without advertising identifiers or cross-site tracking. You can manage cookies in your browser; disabling them may limit some features.

## 12. Changes

The Operator may amend this Policy; the current revision is always on this page. Material changes are communicated by email or in the Service. Continued use means agreement.
