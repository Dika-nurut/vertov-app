# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Vertov?

Vertov is an AI-powered image and video generation service built on ByteDance Seedream and Seedance models. You describe a scene in text, and we produce a visual result in seconds.

## How do I get started?

1. Sign up with your email address.
2. Receive your starter tokens.
3. Go to "Generate" and enter your description.

## What are tokens?

Tokens are Vertov's internal currency. Each generation consumes tokens depending on the selected model and parameters. Your current balance is always visible in the page header.

## Which models are available?

- **Seedream 5.0** — high-detail image generation.
- **Seedance 2.0** — short video generation.

## How do presets work?

Presets ("Ready scenarios") are pre-configured templates with a prompt and parameters. Click "Use" to auto-fill all fields. You can modify the description before generating.

## Where are my images stored?

All results appear in your Gallery. They are private by default. You can publish a result and share a link.

## How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete account. All your personal data will be erased in accordance with applicable law. Tokens reserved for unfinished tasks are refunded automatically.

## How do I contact support?

Email us at **support@vertov.space**. We reply within **5 business days**.
