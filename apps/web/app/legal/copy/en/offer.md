## 1. Definitions

- **Service / Vertov** — the AI image, video and audio generation service available at [vertov.space](https://vertov.space) and related subdomains.
- **User** — a legally capable individual aged 18 or over who has accepted this offer.
- **Tokens** — internal accounting units used to pay for generations. Tokens are not electronic money, cannot be exchanged for cash, and are usable only within the Service.
- **Generation** — a single content-creation operation performed by the Service at the User's request.
- **Subscription** — recurring access to the Service with periodic Token grants.

5.1. Services are paid for with Tokens. Token-pack and Subscription prices are shown at [vertov.space/pricing](https://vertov.space/pricing) and form part of this offer.

5.2. The Token cost of each Generation depends on the chosen model and parameters and is shown before the Generation starts.

5.3. Payment is made in Russian rubles via the Tochka Bank acquiring service. The Provider does not receive or store the User's card data.

5.4. Tokens are added to the User's balance once payment is received. The Provider's obligation is fulfilled when the User is enabled to use the paid Tokens.

5.5. The Provider applies the simplified tax system (USN) and is not a VAT payer. An electronic fiscal receipt is sent to the User under Federal Law No. 54-FZ.

6.1. A Subscription provides recurring access and periodic Token grants per the chosen plan.

6.3. **Cancellation.** The User may cancel auto-renewal at any time under Settings → Billing. Access continues until the end of the paid period; no further charges are made.

6.4. Price changes are communicated before the next charge and apply only to future periods.
