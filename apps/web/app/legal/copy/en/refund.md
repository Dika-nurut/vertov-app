# Refund Policy

_Last updated: May 2026_

## 1. Overview

This Refund Policy sets out the conditions under which Vertov considers refund requests.

## 2. Tokens

- Unused tokens from a purchased pack may be refunded within **14 days** of purchase, provided less than 10% of the pack has been used.
- Bonus tokens (promotional, starter) are non-refundable.

## 3. Subscriptions

- You may cancel your subscription at any time in Settings → Billing.
- On cancellation, access continues until the end of the paid period.
- No partial refund is issued for the current period if the service has already been used.
- If you have not used any generation within 48 hours of payment, a refund is available on request.

## 4. Technical Failures

If a generation fails due to a Service error (status `failed` + code `PROVIDER_ERROR`), tokens are refunded automatically within 10 minutes.

## 5. How to Request a Refund

Send an email to **support@vertov.space** with the subject "Refund" and include:

- your account email;
- date and amount of the payment;
- reason for your request.

We will review your request within **5 business days**.

## 6. Applicable Law

Refunds are processed in accordance with the laws of the Russian Federation, including the Law on Consumer Rights Protection.
